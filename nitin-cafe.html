Arogyajyoti Cafe

At Estate Cafe, indulge in freshness with our tea and coffee brewed from Nitin estate plants. Enjoy a delectable array of snacks and Dongri cuisine while immersing yourself in the serene ambiance. Take the taste of Nitin estate home with you by purchasing our Arogyajyoti products, ensuring you savor the essence of our estate wherever you go.

Arogyajyoti offers a unique experience and products crafted with care and expertise. With a focus on quality and authenticity, our products capture the essence of Nitin estate's rich biodiversity. From aromatic teas and coffees to flavorful spices and herbs, each item is meticulously sourced and curated to deliver a memorable and wholesome experience. Enjoy the goodness of nature with Arogyajyoti, and embark on a journey of wellness and indulgence.

At Arogyajyoti Cafe, we take pride in crafting our snacks, tea, and coffee using ingredients sourced directly from Nitin Estate. Our approach is rooted in sustainability and seasonality, allowing us to create innovative and flavorful dishes based on the availability of plants on the estate. Whether it's a crispy snack or a comforting cup of tea or coffee, each offering is infused with the freshness and essence of Nitin Estate. You can always count on us to deliver a culinary experience that celebrates the bountiful offerings of nature while supporting local sustainability efforts.
