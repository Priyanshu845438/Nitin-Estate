ESTABLISED IN 2015
 

Nitin Estate is a magnificent botanical expanse of land situated 30 kilometers west from the city of Satara, Maharashtra, India. It covers 6 acres of land and has a collection of over 220 plant species displayed in a thematic estate. It has the view of Koyana dam, Shivsagar back-water, and Vasota (Vyagragad) in its periphery. Residing at Falani village, it is an experience crafted to inspire people to create a revolution in the world. We welcome visitors and members to explore our formal garden, spice garden, and coffee plantation all year. The estate is known for numerous species of plants that grow and various types of seasonal flowers and fruits that bloom in this area.
