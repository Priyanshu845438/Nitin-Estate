In the hustle and bustle of our daily lives, we often lose touch with nature's beauty. Picture yourself strolling through the serene surroundings of Nitin Estate—a mesmerizing experience that not only rejuvenates your spirit but also offers an educational insight into a myriad of plant species.

TOUR INSTRUCTIONS

Please help us protect and maintain the Estate by following the instructions

    We care about the environment. Please dispose of waste in recycle bins when appropriate. Do not litter.

    If you recover an item in the estate that does not belong to you, please turn it in to the security point.

    Disposable plastics are strictly not allowed inside the estate.

    Consumption of any kind of food is restricted inside the campus except in the Nitin Estate Café.

    Please leave flowers and plants for everyone to enjoy. Do not collect or remove seeds, flowers, fruits, and vegetables, plant labels, etc.

    Outside food is restricted in the Nitin Estate Cafe.

    Stay on pathways and mowed lawn areas. Do not walk in mulched or planted garden beds.

    The Nitin Estate is a tobacco-free environment. Smoking and tobacco products are not allowed on this property, including all buildings, grounds, restrooms and parking lots.

    For their safety, please supervise children at all times.

    Consumption of alcohol is strictly not allowed in Arogyajyoti Cafe or Nitin Estate area.

    No climbing, running, sports or games.

    Ask the security point for information and directions.

    Be aware that many of the plants grown in the estate may be poisonous to eat or that may cause a skin irritation if touched.

    Do not pick any vegetables and fruits without permission.

    Do not feed or handle the wildlife. It can be harmful to them and you.

    Ensure and maintain complete silence in the Nitin Estate vicinity.

    For safety, please keep your personal items with you at all times. Nitin Estate cannot be held responsible for missing items.
