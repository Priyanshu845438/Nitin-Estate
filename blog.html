About Us

Who We Are

Nitin Estate, nestled in the heart of Satara, Maharashtra, India, is not merely a botanical garden; it is a sanctuary that embodies the essence of beauty, tranquility, and conservation. Established by Nitin Kadam, a visionary with a profound love for nature, the estate stands as a testament to his lifelong commitment to preserving and celebrating the natural world....

KNOW MORE

What We Do at Nitin Estate

At Nitin Estate, our mission is simple yet profound: to foster a deeper connection between people and nature, while promoting sustainability, conservation, and community engagement. Through a range of immersive experiences and initiatives, we strive to inspire, educate, and empower individuals to become stewards of the environment....

KNOW MORE

A Journey Into Nature while exploring the Nitin Estate

Nestled amidst the scenic landscapes of Maharashtra's Satara district lies a hidden gem of natural wonder – Nitin Estate. Situated just 30 kilometers west of Satara city, this magnificent botanical estate offers an escape into the serene embrace of nature. With its roots dating back to 2010, Nitin Estate has evolved into a sanctuary for biodiversity.....

KNOW MORE

Exploring the Botanical Wonders of Nitin Estate

At Nitin Estate, our commitment to preserving and celebrating the diversity of plant life is evident in our expansive botanical collection. With over 220 species and counting, our gardens serve as a vibrant tapestry of colors, shapes, and scents, showcasing the rich tapestry of flora from India and beyond.....

Why Our Work Matters at Nitin Estate

At Nitin Estate, our mission goes beyond merely cultivating gardens; it's about fostering a deeper connection with nature and promoting environmental stewardship. Every plant, every tree, and every visitor to our estate plays a crucial role in our mission to protect and preserve our planet's precious biodiversity.....
